THE MOVE  ·  PRIVACY

Privacy Policy.

The Move is an 18+ social discovery app for the Ithaca community operated by Jacob Berko. This policy explains what information we process, why we use it, and the choices available to you.

Effective August 19, 2026 · Contact business@jacobberko.com

Open The Move support →

Information you provide

We process your email address, display name, adult-eligibility status, account settings, friend connections, saved places, event interests, places you mark as visited, event posts and posters, and reports or support messages you submit.

Age eligibility

Where available, the app asks Apple's Declared Age Range service whether your shared age range meets the 18+ requirement. Apple shares a range rather than your exact birthday. The Move stores only an adult eligibility attestation with your policy acceptance, not the age range or birthday. If Apple provides an under-18 range, the app does not permit access.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate accounts, operate social and discovery features, review events, prevent abuse, respond to reports, maintain security, and provide support. We do not sell personal information or use it for cross-app tracking or targeted advertising.

Visibility

Your display name, avatar, approved event posts, friend count, and aggregate visited-place count may be visible to signed-in users. Your email address, saved places, event interests, individual visited-place history, reports, and block list are private. Blocking prevents either person from viewing or contacting the other through The Move.

Photos and location

The app can access only the photos you intentionally select for an event poster. The Move does not request or collect your device location. If you mark a venue as visited, we store that choice; because each venue has map coordinates, this can reveal a specific place you say you visited.

Service providers and technical data

We use Supabase to provide authentication, database, file storage, and server functions. Service and network providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy commitments and may retain technical data such as IP addresses, approximate location derived from IP, device or browser information, timestamps, and request logs to deliver and secure the service.

Retention

We retain account information while your account is active and as reasonably needed for security, dispute resolution, and legal obligations. Reports may be retained after account deletion when necessary to protect users and document moderation decisions. Other account content is deleted or de-identified when you delete your account.

Your choices

You can edit your display name, remove friends, unsave places, undo visited markers, withdraw event interests, block users, and permanently delete your account in the app. You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Security

We use access controls, encrypted network connections, and restricted administrative access. No system is completely secure, so use a unique password and report suspected account misuse.

Age requirement

The Move is only for people age 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. Report a possible underage account so we can review and remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service or legal requirements change. We will update the effective date and provide additional notice when a change is material.

Contact

Questions and privacy requests can be sent to business@jacobberko.com.
