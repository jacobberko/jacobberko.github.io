THE MOVE  ·  COMMUNITY

Community Guidelines.

Effective August 19, 2026 · Contact business@jacobberko.com

Keep Ithaca safe

The Move is for real community discovery. Treat people with respect, share accurate event information, follow venue rules, and consider the safety and accessibility of everyone involved.

Not allowed

Do not post harassment, threats, hate, explicit sexual content, non-consensual imagery, personal information, scams, impersonation, illegal sales, instructions for dangerous conduct, or content that exploits or endangers a minor.

Event standards

Use a clear title, accurate date and time, real host, valid location, honest pricing, and a poster you have permission to use. Do not promote underage drinking, unsafe capacity, trespassing, impaired driving, or an event that the host or venue has not authorized.

Reports

Use the report option on an event or profile when something violates these rules. Choose the closest reason and add useful context. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services rather than relying on an in-app report.

Blocking

Block anyone you do not want to interact with. Blocking hides each person’s profile and content from the other and removes any friendship or pending request.

Enforcement

We may reject or remove events, warn users, limit features, suspend accounts, or permanently ban accounts. We consider severity, context, history, and risk. Credible threats, child-safety concerns, or illegal activity may be escalated to appropriate authorities.

Appeals

If you believe a moderation decision was a mistake, contact business@jacobberko.com from the email associated with your account and include relevant details.
