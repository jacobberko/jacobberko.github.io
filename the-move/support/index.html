THE MOVE  ·  SUPPORT

App support.

The Move is operated by Jacob Berko.

Email business@jacobberko.com for account help, privacy requests, moderation appeals, or technical support. Include your device model, iOS version, and a concise description of what happened. Never send your password.

Read the Privacy Policy →

Account and email help

  • New accounts must confirm their email address before signing in.
  • Password-reset and confirmation links open The Move on the device where the app is installed.
  • If a link is expired or already used, request a new one from the sign-in screen.
  • Use a unique password with at least eight characters.

Event submissions

New events and posters remain pending until a moderator reviews them. You can view a submission’s status under Profile → My Events. Approval confirms only that the listing passed review; verify important event details directly with the host or venue.

Reports and blocking

Use the report option on an event or profile for suspected policy violations. Reports are not an emergency channel. For immediate danger, call 911 or the appropriate local emergency service. Blocking an account hides both people’s profiles and content from each other and removes any friendship or pending request.

Delete your account

Open Profile → Account, enter your current password, type DELETE, and confirm. Deletion is permanent and removes or de-identifies account content as described in the Privacy Policy. If in-app deletion fails, email support from the address associated with your account.
